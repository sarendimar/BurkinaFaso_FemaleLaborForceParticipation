# BurkinaFaso_FemaleLaborForceParticipation
I wanted to look at how female labor force participation (FLFP) has changed over time in Burkina Faso, with special consideration given to the difference in the gender gap before and after 1987 (assassination of socialist President Thomas Sankara).

My initial hypothesis was that each additional year since 1975 would correlate with some meaningful increase in FLFP, so I ran a simple linear regression to examine the relationship between them. The estimated coefficient for the year variable was positive (β = 0.2219), which suggests an average annual increase of approximately 0.22%. However, this relationship was not statistically significant (p = 0.581), and the model only explained about 2.61% of the observed variation in FLFP (R² = 0.026). 

As for the average difference in the gender gap in labor force participation before and after 1987, my findings were similarly inconclusive. I hypothesized that the gender gap would increase following the Compaoré coup d'etat due to my prior understanding of the differences between the Sankara and Compaoré administrations, but the data showed the opposite. The average gender gap before 1987 was about 52.38% (39.13% f, 91.5% m), and the average gender gap after 1987 was about 12.73% (65.54% f, 78.27% m). It is important to note that there are 12 observations after 1987 and only two before, and also that the 1975 gender gap is the largest recorded, over 60% larger than the next largest gender gap.

It is also important to note that the dataset does not contain observations for every year between 1975 and 2025. I imagine that having annual observations for the time period in question would allow for a more complete analysis of the change in FLFP over time. In addition, I approached the project with the intention of giving special consideration to 1987, but from the participation_trends plot, it seems that there were other years with far more dramatic change.

Further research on these questions would focus on finding data for all of the years not included in this dataset and examining the data on other indicators in the dataset to figure out if there are any other patterns that correlate with trends measured in FLFP. The fact that the model only explains 2.61% of the observed variation in FLFP makes me curious to try and parse out some of whatever makes up the other 97%.
